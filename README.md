# Word Scramble

A browser-based word game. Unscramble the letters before time runs out.

## How to play

1. Choose a difficulty level (Easy / Medium / Hard).
2. Click **Start Game**.
3. Type the unscrambled word and press **Go** or **Enter**.
4. Use **Hint** if you're stuck — or **Skip** to move on.
5. Your best score is saved automatically.

## Difficulty

| Level  | Letters | Time  | Points |
|--------|---------|-------|--------|
| Easy   | 4–5     | 30 s  | 1      |
| Medium | 6–7     | 20 s  | 2      |
| Hard   | 8+      | 15 s  | 3      |

## Running locally

Requires Python 3:

```bash
python3 serve.py
```

Then open [http://localhost:3000](http://localhost:3000) in your browser.
